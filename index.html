01.About us

About us

Monash VISTA designs and builds vision-based sensing systems for smart infrastructure. Based in the Monash Smart Infrastructure (MSI) Lab, we bring together students from engineering, computer science and IT to develop projects across computer vision, AI, IoT and data platforms. We’re a collaborative, enthusiastic team building hardware and software solutions for real-world problems in transport, smart cities, healthcare and beyond.
